# Mission notes -- <sub-project name>

Write findings, decisions, and open questions here as you work, as they
happen -- not reconstructed at the end of a session. See the root
`CLAUDE.md` (or its equivalent for this sub-project) for standing rules;
read it first.

**Lists, not paragraphs** (see RULE #4). One section per dated finding:

    ## YYYY-MM-DD: short headline of what this section is about

    - what happened / what was found, as a bullet, not a paragraph
    - what it affects
    - what's still open, if anything

**Archive superseded sections** (see RULE #5) into this file's own
`MISSION_NOTES_ARCHIVE.md` once a later section fully replaces an earlier
one on the same question -- keep only the current account live here.

Facts this sub-project's thread discovers that another parallel thread
needs to know NOW, not just whenever it next reads a shared doc, get
delivered directly (see `docs/cross-mission-coordination.md`) -- writing
it here is necessary but not sufficient.
